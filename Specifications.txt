Project: Hydrogen Compressor Upgrade
Document Type: Technical Specification
Version: 1.2
Date: 2026-02-10

1. Scope
This document defines requirements for upgrading the hydrogen compressor system in Unit B.
The target is to improve availability, reduce vibration trips, and increase flow stability.

2. Design Requirements
- Rated flow: 12,000 Nm3/h
- Suction pressure: 18 bar
- Discharge pressure: 42 bar
- Max vibration velocity: 4.5 mm/s RMS
- Bearing temperature alarm: 85 C
- Bearing temperature trip: 95 C

3. Instrumentation
- Vibration probes on DE and NDE bearings
- Redundant pressure transmitters at suction and discharge
- RTDs for bearing and lube oil temperatures
- Flow meter with 1-second sampling interval

4. Risk and Compliance
- Risk: Seal gas contamination may cause premature wear.
- Mitigation: Install dual-stage filtration and weekly gas quality checks.
- Compliance: IEC 60034 and plant safety standard PS-118 shall be followed.
- Cybersecurity controls for PLC communication must align with internal policy.

5. Commissioning
- Pre-start checks for lube oil pressure, cooling water flow, and interlock logic.
- Perform no-load test for 2 hours and full-load test for 8 hours.
- Acceptance criteria: no trips, vibration < 4.5 mm/s, stable discharge pressure.

6. Maintenance Strategy
- Monthly condition monitoring
- Quarterly alignment verification
- Annual overhaul planning based on vibration trend and oil analysis
